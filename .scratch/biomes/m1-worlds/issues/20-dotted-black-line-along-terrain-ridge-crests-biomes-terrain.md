## Report

During the MPM lava work (#74), a dotted black line was seen along a terrain ridge crest in the volcano demo. It shows up in daylight frames at a distance. It is still there with the lava surface hidden, so it comes from the threejs-biomes terrain (or shadows from the sky sun), not from the particle passes.

## Repro

Open the MPM lava demo in daylight with the default camera. Look at the ridge line near the top of the frame, before or after lava is emitted.

## Suspected causes (unconfirmed)

- Shadow acne or peter-panning on steep terrain at grazing sun angles (cascaded shadow map bias).
- Normal or slope discontinuities between terrain LOD rings or tiles at the crest.
- Detail texture or rock-mask aliasing along a sharp slope threshold. That would break the no-jaggies rule (#54).
