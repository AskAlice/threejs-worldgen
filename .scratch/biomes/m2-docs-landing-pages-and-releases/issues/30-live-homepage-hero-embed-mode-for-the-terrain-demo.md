## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** An `?embed` mode for the planet terrain demo, and the homepage hero using it as a live viewport over the still image, like the grass lander.

**Desired behavior:**
- In embed mode, the demo hides its panel and HUD and runs a slow, deterministic orbit.
- It posts ready, play and pause messages compatible with the grass lander's iframe protocol, and pauses rendering when hidden or off-screen.
- The homepage shows the still image until the iframe reports ready, then crossfades to it.
- It falls back to the still image without WebGPU, with reduced motion, or on small screens.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The homepage hero is live on WebGPU browsers and stays a still image otherwise.
- [ ] Rendering pauses while the hero is off-screen (no GPU work).
- [ ] Headless check: no console errors, and the hero is ready within 10 s.

**Out of scope:** changes to terrain visuals.
